Last updated 2026-10-14.

Everything you type into the calculators stays in your browser. We don't have accounts, a database or tracking cookies. Your inputs appear in the page address (URL) only so you can share a result; that link is not sent to us.

If you choose dark or light mode, that choice is saved in your browser's local storage.

If ads or analytics are added in the future, this page will be updated first to explain the cookies they use and how to opt out.

The email signup is not connected yet and stores nothing. When it opens, your address will be used only to send updates, and you can unsubscribe at any time.
