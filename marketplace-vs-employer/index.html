Marketplace vs employer plan calculator

Income, household size and two prices. That’s it.

How it works: We apply the IRS affordability percentage to your income, then the premium tax credit table for your poverty-level band, using the benchmark silver plan.

General education, not insurance, tax or legal advice. Simplified math (deductible, then coinsurance, up to the out-of-pocket max). It can't see your plan's network, drug list or exact rules. Check your plan's Summary of Benefits and Coverage.

Marketplace inputs

Modified AGI: roughly your adjusted gross income.

Benchmark = second-cheapest silver plan for your ZIP on HealthCare.gov (“See plans and prices”).
