# EnrollMath

Free HSA vs PPO break-even calculator with 2026–2027 IRS limits, plus COBRA cost and employer plan vs marketplace comparison with a tax credit estimate.

**Live site: [https://enrollmath.com/](https://enrollmath.com/)** (free, no signup, runs in your browser)

## Tools
- [HSA vs PPO calculator](https://enrollmath.com/hsa-vs-ppo/): Which plan costs less for you: the HSA plan or the PPO?
- [Compare 2–4 plans](https://enrollmath.com/compare-plans/): Put up to four plans side by side at low, medium and high use.
- [COBRA cost calculator](https://enrollmath.com/cobra-calculator/): What COBRA really costs, and whether a marketplace plan is cheaper.
- [Marketplace vs job plan](https://enrollmath.com/marketplace-vs-employer/): Do you qualify for a premium tax credit, or is your job plan “affordable”?
- [HSA per-paycheck calculator](https://enrollmath.com/hsa-paycheck-calculator/): How much to put in your HSA each paycheck to max it out.

## Data sources
HSA and HDHP limits come from IRS Revenue Procedures and Notices, the affordability percentage and premium tax credit tables from IRS Revenue Procedures, and the federal poverty guidelines from HHS. COBRA and marketplace rules follow Healthcare.gov and Department of Labor guidance. Each guide cites its source and year. Your premiums, deductibles and usage are your inputs.
