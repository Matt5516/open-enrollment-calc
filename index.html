Official figures used and sources

Checked .

  • 2026 HSA limits $4,400 self / $8,750 family; HDHP minimum deductible $1,700 / $3,400; HDHP out-of-pocket max $8,500 / $17,000. IRS Rev. Proc. 2025-19: irs.gov/pub/irs-drop/rp-25-19.pdf
  • 2027 HSA limits $4,500 self / $9,000 family; HDHP minimum deductible $1,750 / $3,500; HDHP out-of-pocket max $8,700 / $17,400. IRS Rev. Proc. 2026-24: irs.gov/pub/irs-drop/rp-26-24.pdf
  • HSA catch-up contribution of $1,000 at age 55+: Internal Revenue Code §223(b)(3); see IRS Publication 969: irs.gov/publications/p969
  • Employer coverage affordability (required contribution percentage) 9.96% for 2026 (Rev. Proc. 2025-25: rp-25-25.pdf) and 10.22% for 2027 (Rev. Proc. 2026-26: rp-26-26.pdf).
  • COBRA premiums up to 102% of plan cost (150% during disability extension), generally 18 months for job loss: U.S. Department of Labor, A Worker's Guide to Health Benefits Under COBRA
  • Employee FICA rate 7.65% (6.2% Social Security + 1.45% Medicare): ssa.gov. Some states tax HSA contributions; adjust your tax rate if so.
